# Qualification / Decision Reason Codes v1.0.4

Reason codes are deterministic product facts. AI translates them; it does not invent the decision reason.

## Common qualification / block codes

- `NET_BELOW_MIN`
- `CAPACITY_BELOW_MIN`
- `NO_EXECUTABLE_CAPACITY`
- `INPUT_STALE`
- `FEE_FACT_STALE`
- `INSTRUMENT_INACTIVE`
- `INSTRUMENT_MAPPING_UNVERIFIED`
- `DELTA_MISMATCH_EXCEEDED`
- `SLIPPAGE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`
- `MARGIN_EVIDENCE_MISSING`
- `MARGIN_STRESS_BUFFER_FAILURE`
- `LIQUIDATION_DISTANCE_HARD_BLOCK`
- `VENUE_RISK_UNREVIEWED_OR_BLOCKED`
- `SETTLEMENT_ASSET_RISK_UNREVIEWED_OR_BLOCKED`
- `PORTFOLIO_LIMIT_BREACH`
- `CONNECTOR_HEALTH_BLOCK`
- `KILL_SWITCH_ACTIVE`
