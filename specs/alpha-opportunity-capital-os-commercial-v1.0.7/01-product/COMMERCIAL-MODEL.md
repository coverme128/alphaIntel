# Commercialization Boundary

Commercial V1 is optimized for owner use. Future revenue can be layered on top of validated execution economics.

Potential future revenue components:

- subscription for opportunity intelligence;
- professional/API tier;
- broker rebates where a venue program and jurisdiction permit;
- execution or platform fees where legally permitted;
- enterprise/private deployment.

None of these are required to validate the core product.

## Product integrity rule

The system MUST NOT optimize recommendations to maximize broker rebates or execution fees. Venue economics may be included in cost calculations, but opportunity ranking must use user economic outcome first.
