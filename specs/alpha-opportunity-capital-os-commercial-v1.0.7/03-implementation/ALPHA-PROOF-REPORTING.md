# Alpha Proof Reporting

The primary screen/report must answer, in order:

1. Is economic status UNPROVEN / positive / validated / invalidated?
2. How much reconciled net P&L was actually earned?
3. What would the shadow benchmark have earned?
4. What is the excess economic value after product run cost?
5. What drawdown and execution loss were incurred?
6. How concentrated is the profit?
7. How much expected P&L survived into realized P&L?
8. Is the current evidence enough to continue, expand, or stop?

Never lead with opportunity count or headline APR.

## Mandatory labels

- REPLAY, PAPER or LIVE;
- observation dates/days;
- capital/NAV basis;
- benchmark name/version;
- strategy/config hash;
- annualization sample label;
- data coverage warnings.
